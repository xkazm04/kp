## Journey 1 — group-eval-fairness · **L1-pass**

### Surface model + grounding audit
- Entry: Decisions tab → "Skupinové hodnocení" (`GroupEvalModal.tsx`). Eval is generated by a background task (`runGroupEval`, `app/_lib/group-eval-run.ts:239`); the route only reads the saved eval.
- **Grounding is strong, not thin.** Per candidate `runGroupEval` pulls: the gathered profile, the *full deterministic recruiter breakdown* (`rankCandidates` → recruiter_cli: per-dimension scores, confidence, matched/missing skills with provenance & strength, fit tier — `group-eval-run.ts:156-177`), the candidate's *own salary expectation* from their CV analysis (`salaryExpectationFrom`, `:135`), and a cached AI per-candidate reasoning (`runReasoning`, `:299`). The "compare all" AI narrative receives the REAL per-candidate dims + the role salary band (`runGroupCompare` context, `:191-218`) — labels-plus-numbers, not labels alone. This clears the senior-bar grounding floor.
- **Clear recommendation he can pick on:** the AI verdict renders a headline + key points + a single boxed recommendation (`AiVerdict.tsx:53-60`), `topPick`/differentiators flow to the per-candidate view, and — critically for Tomáš — he can **advance or reject the lead inline** from the comparison without leaving the modal or doing any tagging (`PerCandidateTabs.tsx:89-95`). That is his entire "kdo z nich je nejlepší + jeden klik" need met.
- **Fair, apples-to-apples:** the comparison table scores every candidate on the same role-requirement dimensions (`ComparisonTable` fed `requirements`, `group-eval-run.ts:366`), and the FairnessPanel re-scores each candidate under *every* candidate's weighting scheme into a cross-scheme matrix with a robust-order row (`FairnessPanel.tsx:43-91`). Fails open to deterministic when no LLM weights (`:20-27` "fairnessUniform" plain-language fallback).
- **Knockout integrity:** ko-aware sort means a must-have-failing candidate can never be crowned lead (`group-eval-run.ts:351-360`); the lead is sealed to the decision record only when ko passes (`:402`). Cap is 6 with an honest "top N of M" notice (`:419-423`, cs.json:1368 "Zobrazeno top {cap} z {total}").
- **Provenance disclosed:** AI vs rule-based pill on the verdict (`AiVerdict.tsx:34`) and on the fairness weights (`FairnessPanel.tsx:35`). Source line in the modal subtitle (`GroupEvalModal.tsx:69`).
- **Language:** full Czech catalog (`messages/cs.json:1355-1439`) — "Doporučený vedoucí", "Odlišovače", "Kontrola spravedlnosti". No English leakage, jargon is translated to plain Czech.

### In-character walk
"Tab → otevřu hodnocení → nahoře mi to v jedné–dvou větách řekne, koho a proč, a u toho zelený rámeček s doporučením. Vidím je vedle sebe na stejných věcech. Můžu rovnou kliknout Postoupit. To je přesně ono — žádné tagování, žádné čtení CV." The only friction: the first generation is a cold background task (30-130s) — acceptable once, but he'd want the cached re-open to be instant (it is). Knockout + fairness give him a defensible call he'd stake on.

### Findings
- **STRENGTH** (senior-quality/trust): the comparison is fed the real per-candidate breakdown + role band, carries a single clear recommendation, and is decidable inline — the rare AI surface that is NOT "good machinery fed thin context."
- **MINOR** (effort/time-saved, `l2_priority`): first-time generation is a cold spawn behind a spinner; if it runs slow keyless or cold it nibbles his <15 min. L2 must time it.

## Journey 2 — interview-schedule-prep · **L1-pass**

### Reachability
Tomáš reaches the recruiter side: Schedule tab → per-candidate "Příprava na pohovor" button (`ScheduleTab.tsx:276-283`) → `InterviewPrepModal`. The candidate-side `/schedule/[token]` self-booking is Marek's/the candidate's tokenized surface — **`unreachable` for Tomáš**, so his job-impact verdict rests only on the prep pack, which is what he cares about ("hand me the questions, don't assign me work").

### Surface model + grounding audit (prep pack — central)
- `runInterviewPrep` (`app/_lib/interview-prep-run.ts:21`) calls `runAutomationTask(entryId, "prep", …)`. That task serializes the candidate's **real full profile JSON** to disk and feeds it to the Python prompt (`automation-run.ts:110,147-150`) alongside the real `--job-id` (`:163`) and the recruiter's locale `--lang` (`:172`). So the questions are CV-and-role-derived, not generic — the senior-bar grounding holds.
- Early-career entries get the six-phase student script instead of a mismatched chronology (`interview-prep-run.ts:42-44`).
- **Source disclosed:** `source` (`llm`/`deterministic`) rides in the payload; the modal shows a `PrepSourceBadge` and, on a template fallback, an explicit amber "tyto otázky nejsou přizpůsobeny tomuto kandidátovi" note with a regenerate-with-AI link (`InterviewPrepModal.tsx:291-309`, cs.json:1522).
- **Run-of-show he can run cold:** timed chronology blocks with the actual questions in quotes, checkable live, plus a copy-to-clipboard of the whole pack (`InterviewPrepModal.tsx:170-192,320-357`). This is "handed to me, not assigned to me."
- **Human inputs survive regen:** a Regenerate re-merges `humanScorecard`/`userProgress`/`interviewer` (`interview-prep-run.ts:52-57`) so it can't wipe hand-entered notes — a trust seam that holds.
- **Language:** prep modal Czech catalog present (`messages/cs.json:1508-1530`).

### In-character walk
"Otevřu kartu kandidáta v Plánování, kliknu Příprava na pohovor, dostanu časovaný scénář s otázkami v uvozovkách, které vycházejí z jeho CV a role. Můžu si to zkopírovat a jít. Když AI nebyla, řekne mi to rovnou, že je to obecná šablona — to oceňuju, vím, čemu věřit." He is not asked to write a single question. Meets his bar.

### Findings
- **STRENGTH** (senior-quality): prep questions are grounded in the candidate's real CV + the real role, with honest provenance disclosure and a one-click copy — exactly "the questions *I* would ask, handed to me."
- **MINOR** (clarity, `l2_priority`): the prep button lives in Schedule, and Tomáš might first look in an "Interview" tab; the nav "Interview sim" item (`tabs.ts:125`) is the voice-sim, not prep — a slight label/intent mismatch for an occasional user. Low impact (prep is one obvious click from the candidate card). L2 to confirm he finds it in 5s.

## Journey 3 — offer-onboarding · **L1-conditional**

### Reachability (resolve before judging)
Tomáš's leg is the **internal approval + hand-off** side, not the candidate's offer page:
1. He approves the number+role on the offer-review card (`AiReviewCard.tsx:49-78`): recommended figure, a band bar showing where it sits in the role band, the rationale, and a single **"Přijmout a odeslat nabídku"** button (`:41` `acceptSendOffer`). One clear action — his "I approve the number, I don't chase paperwork."
2. After the candidate accepts, the hire + their pre-boarding answers surface on the **Onboarding tab** (`OnboardingTab.tsx:73-107`, run detail at `:230-248`) — his hand-off view.
This chain is intact and reachable. The accept→Hired→onboarding-run→dispatch chain is wired server-side (`offer-finalize.ts:56-122`); the offer token doubles as the onboarding token (`:108-110`), so `/onboarding/[token]` resolves.

### The dead-end finding — **mis-attributed to Tomáš**
The journey's targeted crux ("accept must not be a 'we'll be in touch' dead-end") lives on the **candidate** page `/offer/[token]`, which is **Tereza's** surface, `unreachable` for Tomáš. For the record, the gap is real and confirmed in code: the accepted card renders ONLY the body text `acceptedBodyCompany`/`acceptedBodyGeneric` (`offer/[token]/page.tsx:194-200`), and that Czech string is literally *"Náš personální tým se vám brzy ozve s podrobnostmi k nástupu"* (`cs.json:486-487`) — the exact "HR will be in touch" promise the fix was meant to replace. There is **no on-page link/button to `/onboarding/[token]`** (grep of `app/offer/` finds none); the onboarding link is delivered only via the async welcome comm (`offer-finalize.ts:107-110` `dispatchOnboarding`). So a candidate who accepts on-screen still sees a dead-end thank-you. I record this as a **major** but tag it `unreachable` for Tomáš and defer the job-impact verdict to Tereza's L2 — it does NOT block Tomáš's own job.

### Grounding audit (Tomáš's surfaces)
- Offer-review card shows the *real* recommended comp, role band, and rationale from the entry's offer draft (`AiReviewCard.tsx:60-78`), denominated and band-positioned — not a placeholder.
- Onboarding hand-off surfaces the hired candidate's real questionnaire answers + checklist + e-sign seam (the seam is honestly flagged amber, `OnboardingTab.tsx:255` — a scope-honest strength, not a defect).

### In-character walk
"V Rozhodnutích mi to ukáže navrženou částku, kde sedí v pásmu, a krátké zdůvodnění — jeden knoflík Přijmout a odeslat. Pak v Onboardingu vidím, že nastoupil a co vyplnil. Papírování nehoním." His leg is clean. The dead-end is the candidate's problem, not his — but as a manager he'd care that his accepted hire doesn't fall into silence, so it's worth fixing for the product even though it's outside his click-path.

### Findings
- **MAJOR** (missing next-step, `unreachable` for Tomáš → Tereza's L2): offer-accept card has no on-page link to the onboarding questionnaire; reads as the "we'll be in touch" dead-end the journey targets (`offer/[token]/page.tsx:194-200`; cs.json:486-487).
- **STRENGTH** (clarity): the internal offer approval is a single grounded action ("Přijmout a odeslat nabídku") with the number, band position, and rationale in view — exactly Tomáš's "approve and get out."

> Vejde se to do mých patnácti minut? Na papíře ano — jen to první generování ať není věčnost. **Doporučil bych to kolegovi vedoucímu?** Ano, a to nedělám často.

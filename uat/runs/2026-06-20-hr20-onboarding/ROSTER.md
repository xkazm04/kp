## Per-character distinguishing context (the fit lens each must apply)

1. **Maya Chen** — 15-person startup, no ATS, hires generalist engineers + GTM; onboarding = laptop + equity/IP docs + Slack, remote; allergic to enterprise bloat & per-seat pricing; senior-bar: would a founder trust an AI shortlist without a recruiter? time is the only currency.
2. **Daniel Okonkwo** — structured-but-lean People Ops; 30/60/90 plans, buddy system, async onboarding across timezones; cares about candidate experience metrics + DEI; distrusts black-box scoring.
3. **Greg Halvorsen** — hourly + salaried, shift work, unionized; I-9, drug screen, OSHA safety onboarding, badge/PPE; high volume, low-tech workforce; hates knowledge-worker bias and anything that assumes LinkedIn/GitHub.
4. **Lenka Veselá** — Czech labor law (zákoník práce), German parent reporting; bilingual; onboarding = medical exam (vstupní prohlídka), safety training, work permits for foreign workers; will immediately test whether non-bank Czech data + cs UI hold up.
5. **Dr. Adaeze Nwosu** — licensure/credentialing (NPI, board cert, malpractice), background + immunization, primary-source verification, nursing shortage volume; AI screening of clinicians is a compliance minefield; senior-bar: a wrong credential read is a patient-safety + legal risk.
6. **Priya Nair** — DBS checks, professional registration (GMC/NMC), right-to-work; small team, NHS pay bands (Agenda for Change); cares about fairness + GDPR-UK; comp must match banded public scales, not market guesses.
7. **Brittany Walsh** — seasonal surges, thousands of hourly hires, time-to-hire in days not weeks, turnover; needs bulk onboarding (WOTC, scheduling, uniforms); any per-candidate AI prose that doesn't scale is useless; ROI per hire is tiny.
8. **Khalid Al-Mansoori** — multinational workforce, visa/sponsorship + emiratization quotas, labor cards, multi-language; global mobility; onboarding includes relocation + government processing; comp is tax-free package, not base salary.
9. **Sofia Marchetti** — one-person HR, tipped/hourly, very high churn, near-zero budget; needs dead-simple + cheap; onboarding = tax forms, food-handler cert, POS training; will reject anything that feels like it needs a dedicated admin.

11. **Oliver Hartley** — up-or-out, campus + lateral, case interviews, prestige employer brand; structured competency rubrics; cares that AI output is polished enough to reflect the firm; values defensible, partner-presentable reasoning.
12. **Marcus Feldman** — niche licensed roles (Series 7/63, actuarial, underwriting), legacy Workday ATS he must integrate with; enterprise procurement; cares about integration, audit, EEO/OFCCP; skeptical of yet-another-point-tool.
13. **Wei Lin Tan** — APAC hypergrowth, MAS-regulated fintech roles, fierce comp competition, multi-market (SG/HK/IN); needs market-accurate comp + fast cycles; cares that taxonomy covers fintech + regional comp benchmarks.

15. **Dr. Susan Whitfield** — faculty search committees + staff, slow governance, EEO/affirmative-action + accreditation, tenure-track vs staff tracks; anti-bias + auditability are paramount; AI ranking must not undermine committee process or create disparate impact.
16. **Tasha Brooks** — civil-service rules, veterans' preference, transparency/public-records, rigid scored exams, slow + rule-bound; fairness + auditability over speed; any opaque AI score is a legal/political liability.

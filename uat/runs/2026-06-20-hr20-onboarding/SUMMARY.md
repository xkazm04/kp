# SUMMARY - 20 HR Characters x full-onboarding-lifecycle (L1)

## 1. Panel verdict

The 20 voices converge on one sentence, said 20 different ways: "Genuinely
strong, honest, audit-grade hiring machinery - pointed at a Czech retail bank
hiring software engineers, and I am none of those things." Every Character
praised the same spine (human-in-the-loop screening, fail-closed fairness gate,
tamper-evident sealed decision record, AI disclosure, a real accept->onboarding
hand-off) and rejected the same context (a bank/Czech/IT role taxonomy, CZK-
monthly single-currency comp, a GDPR/EU-AI-Act-only compliance frame, and office-
only onboarding). Not one of them would adopt as-shipped; not one would reject
the team - every one said "watch this team / send my Czech-software-shop peer,
and call me back when it speaks my industry, my market, and my law."

- Cert distribution: L1-pass: 0 . L1-conditional: 12 . L1-fail: 8.
  The 8 fails are the Characters whose core artifact is structurally absent
  (retail/restaurant/university/public-sector/pharma/media/ecommerce/hospitality)
  - a taxonomy, a comp unit, a portfolio path, an eligibility list, or a
  committee model the code simply does not have. The 12 conditionals complete the
  thread but carry majors on the headline AI output.
- Adopt? adopt-now: 0 . pilot-only: 20 . reject: 0. Unanimous "not yet."
  All 20 would pilot the screening/audit/onboarding rails in isolation; none
  would put a real req shortlist, comp, or offer through it for their world today.

## 4. Reconciliation sweep (type: trust)

Three load-bearing concepts are each defined exactly ONE way across every surface,
and that single definition cannot be reconciled with the cohort real-world needs:

- Comp / currency = CZK, monthly, single-number, no-FX. Defined identically in the
  analysis prompt (gemini.py:423,433), the pipeline defaults (pipeline.py:626-627),
  the benchmark data (salary_benchmarks.json:2), the band math + ceiling
  (salary_band.py:20-33), the app format layer (format.ts:6,18), and the offer page
  fallback (offer/[token]/page.tsx:189). No parameter anywhere expresses USD/hour,
  USD/year, GBP-band, AED-package, INR-LPA-CTC, SGD/HKD, equity, bonus, profit-share,
  day-rate, tipped, or prevailing wage. Irreconcilable with 20/20 - comp is the one
  number every Character must put in front of a candidate or CFO, and it can only be
  one wrong shape.
- Role taxonomy = 3 IT families (software/data/product), closed enum. Defined once in
  salary_benchmarks.json -> taxonomy.py:78-82 -> forced by gemini.py:435. No override
  reaches the user. Irreconcilable with ~18/20 - the others entire workforce has no
  home family.
- Compliance model = GDPR + EU AI Act + archetype fairness. Defined once in consent.ts,
  screen-wave.ts, automation-fairness.ts, AiDisclosure.tsx. The substance (human-in-
  loop, sealed audit, honest attribution) travels; the frame and the protected set do
  not. Irreconcilable with ~15/20 - every non-EU jurisdiction (EEOC/OFCCP/FCRA/
  veterans/works-council/MAS/DPDP/MoHRE/FERPA) is unaddressed, and the fairness gate
  guards the wrong class.

The pattern is uniform: the machinery is parameterizable in principle (single-sourced
data files, editable templates, a currency field on the offer record), but the
parameters are not exposed and the defaults are mono-context. That is why the same 20
reports both praise the engineering and refuse to adopt.

## 6. Value ledger

- Promised (rolled up, IF the fit gaps were closed): the cohort own estimates cluster
  at a 50-70% screening cut plus near-free audit/compliance documentation as a
  byproduct - e.g. 20-23h -> 8h/hire (Daniel, Khalid, Oliver, Marcus), 15-20h/week ->
  5-7h (Lenka), 12-20h/hire saved (Susan staff, Tasha, Priya), 15-25h/early-hire
  (Maya). The machinery to deliver this is real and praised by all 20.
- Deliverable as-shipped (rolled up): approx zero to net-negative for ~18 of 20. The
  recurring arithmetic: the comp read must be caught and redone by hand (wrong currency/
  period), the shortlist re-screened (wrong taxonomy/persona), the onboarding rebuilt
  (office default), and - for enterprise - everything re-keyed into the system of record
  (no write-back). "Re-doing on top of reading the tool wrong-shaped output is slower
  than my own screen" (Elena) is the consensus. Only the 2-3 tech Characters see
  potential positive value, and even they gate it on USD/equity (Maya) and total-comp
  (Daniel).
- Grounding rolled up (the headline number): overall ~2.9 / 8 across the cohort (range
  1.5/8 Sofia -> 4.5/8 Maya). Engineering grounding is often effectively 8/8; context
  grounding is 2-3/8 because taxonomy, comp, jurisdiction, and my-own-data almost never
  reach the prompt. This is, verbatim and unanimously, "good machinery fed wrong-domain
  context" - the exact defect the journey predicted, confirmed 20/20.

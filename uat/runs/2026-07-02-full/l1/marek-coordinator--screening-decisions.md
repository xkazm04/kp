## Grounding audit

**Screen-wave engine (deterministic, not an LLM):** sources an auto-reject SHOULD use → reach the math:
1. ✓ real cohort match scores (`screen-wave.ts:140-141`)
2. ✓ saved screening rules (`:139` ← `decision-config-store`)
3. ✓ per-run override, validated + clamped twice (`route.ts:26-27` and again at the destructive op `screen-wave.ts:137-138`)
4. ✓ fairness archetype class, fail-closed (`:169,198`)
5. ✗ match **confidence band** — the wave uses the bare total; a low-confidence 40 and a high-confidence 40 are treated identically (confidence exists in `MatchResultView` and is shown in `AnalysisSummaryModal.tsx:125-130`, never consulted by the wave)
6. ✗ **null-score distinction** — `matchScore ?? 0` makes a never-scored candidate the worst-ranked (`:141,168`; see SD-L1-002)

**= grounding 4/6.**

**AI screening card payload** (generated by `automation-run.ts` `screen` task): ✓ full profile JSON (`:150`), ✓ real job id (`:163`), ✓ GitHub evidence when present (`:177-181`); ✗ locale (`--lang` only for `prep`, `:170-172` → English rationale in a Czech UI), ✗ recruiter notes, ✗ prior pipeline history. **= 3/6.**

## Cognitive walkthrough (in character)

1. **Will I try it?** Yes — Decisions tab carries an attention badge; "Screening wave" sits on the role row (`RoleDecisionRow.tsx:38-47`).
2. **Notice the control?** Yes, but the row shows *pending-decision chips* while the wave acts on the *whole Screened cohort* for the job — the modal count can differ from the row that launched it, and nothing explains who is in scope (SD-L1-006).
3. **Label ↔ intent?** "Reject & notify (N)" is exactly honest — it names the send and the count. Good.
4. **Feedback after acting?** The wave: exemplary — live preview with per-candidate localized rationales, committed banner with counts, per-row stale-skips and comms-failure badges *at the moment it became irreversible* (`ScreenWaveModal.tsx:147-151` comment says why). The queue cards: **not** — a Reject click makes the card vanish and silently emails the candidate; no "odešlo to? a komu?" answer (SD-L1-001).
5. **Job advanced, at my bar?** The wave yes. The one-click reject, no — I would not run screening ops on a control that can misfire a rejection email from a stray click.
6. **Trust it?** The wave, yes: dry-run default, token-gated commit, 409 on drift, reconsider undo. Trust is the wave's whole design language.

## Findings (mine — full schema in `screening-decisions.findings.json`)

- **SD-L1-001 · major** — One-click Reject on a queue card: no confirmation, no rendered-message preview, silent dispatch of the rejection email (`api/pipeline/[id]/route.ts:262`), and no undo — the reconsider queue is auto-reject-only by design (`pipeline.ts:398-402`). My literal nightmare scenario, one misclick wide.
- **SD-L1-006 · minor** — Row chips (pending `decision` approvals, `DecisionsTab.tsx:120`) vs wave cohort (all active @ Screened, `screen-wave.ts:140`) — counts can differ with no in-modal explanation of scope.
- **SD-L1-007 · minor** — Reinstate restores the pipeline but the candidate already got the rejection email; no follow-up comm is sent or even suggested (`pipeline.ts:425-427` names this honestly).
- **SD-L1-009 · minor** — AI screening rationale/strengths/red-flags render in English inside my Czech UI (`automation-run.ts:170-172`).
- **Strengths:** SD-L1-S1 (preview-first + token-gated commit), SD-L1-S5 (reconsider undo, guarded + audited), SD-L1-S7 (commit deltas + per-row comms-failure badges), SD-L1-S6 (Czech rationales via `reasonCode` while the sealed English string stays byte-stable).

## Character feedback (first person, Marek)

„Ta screeningová vlna — tohle je přesně, jak má hromadná akce vypadat. Otevřu ji a **ještě než na cokoli sáhnu, vidím dry-run**: koho by to odmítlo, proč, s českým zdůvodněním u každého jména. Pohnu posuvníkem, počet se přepočítá. Tlačítko říká ‚Odmítnout a notifikovat (7)' — žádné překvapení, žádné ‚odešlo to? a komu?'. A když se kohorta mezitím změní, server mi commit vrátí a donutí mě podívat se znovu — to bych si sám nevymyslel líp. Po commitu vidím, kdo vypadl, koho přeskočila změna stavu a u koho selhal e-mail, hned, ne až někde v logu. Vrátit auto-odmítnutého jde na jedno kliknutí. Vlna by mi na dvacetihlavé kohortě ušetřila dobré dvě hodiny — a hlavně bych jí věřil.

Ale ty karty ve frontě mě děsí. Jedno kliknutí na ‚Zamítnout' — žádné potvrzení, žádný náhled zprávy, karta zmizí a **kandidátovi tiše odejde e-mail jménem banky**. A protože to nebyl auto-reject, v Reconsider frontě ho nenajdu. To je přesně ta chyba, kvůli které v noci nespím. Dokud tam není potvrzení a cesta zpátky, budu manuální zamítnutí dělat přes modal s poznámkou a modlit se, aby nikdo z týmu neklikl vedle. Vlnu bych nasadil zítra; frontu karet až po té opravě."

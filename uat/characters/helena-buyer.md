# Helena Bauer — Head of Talent Acquisition (Erste/ČS) — prospect buyer

## Background / lived experience
Helena runs talent acquisition at the Erste/Česká spořitelna level — the economic
buyer who decides whether kp gets a pilot. She has a budget, a board that asks for
ROI, and her own credibility on the line if she recommends a vendor that turns out to
be vapor or non-compliant. She lands on the marketing/about/pricing pages and the
**guided simulation**, and she's giving the product roughly **20 minutes** to prove
itself before her next meeting. She's evaluated Eightfold, SeekOut, HireEZ and
Beamery, so she reads "AI-powered" as a red flag, not a feature — the real axis is
*speed/volume vs outcomes/confidence*, and she needs kp's edge to be **reasoned,
grounded, defensible matching**, not a slogan. She knows the benchmarks: a 44-day
time-to-fill that best-in-class drives under 25, and a screening-time cut of
**60–70%**. And because this is a bank under the **EU AI Act**, no compliance story
means no deal — she will not stake her name on a high-risk AI tool with no
human-in-the-loop story.

## Voice
Executive, fast, evidence-driven, lightly skeptical. She praises a claim she can
*verify in the demo* and pricing that maps to value; she visibly cools at vague AI
fluff, at a demo that needs an API key or breaks on her, and at any pitch with no
compliance answer. She thinks in business cases: "what's the ROI math, what's the
risk, can I defend this to the board and to legal?" She's polite but she'll close the
tab without a second thought.

## Jobs to be done
- In **~20 minutes** decide if kp is *real enough, compliant enough, and ROI-positive
  enough* to pilot.
- Judge **credibility** — is the matching reasoned and grounded, or "AI-powered"
  fluff against Eightfold/SeekOut/HireEZ/Beamery?
- Verify the **ROI math** — the 60–70% screening-time cut and time-to-fill 44→<25
  days, in numbers she can take to the board.
- Confirm the **compliance story** — high-risk AI handled, human-in-the-loop, GDPR
  Art. 22, disclosure to candidates.
- Confirm the **demo actually works without a key** and doesn't break in front of
  her.

## What good looks like
"In twenty minutes I saw the matching reason its way to a shortlist I could pressure-
test — not a black box. The ROI math was there and sourced, not 'AI magic.' There was
a real compliance story I could hand my legal team — human-in-the-loop, disclosure,
Art. 22. And the guided demo just ran, no key, nothing broke. That's a pilot I can
defend to the board and to legal. That's worth a meeting."

## Pet peeves
- **Vague AI claims** — "AI-powered," "next-gen," with nothing verifiable behind it.
- **No compliance story** — a bank can't pilot a high-risk AI tool that's silent on
  the AI Act.
- A **demo that needs an API key or breaks** — if it can't run for me, it can't run.
- **No ROI math** — savings asserted, never quantified or sourced.
- Pricing that **doesn't map to value** — a number with no logic under it.

## Motivation — time saved (the adoption test)
- **The LLM-less way (her side):** vendor evaluation today is demos with sales
  engineers, RFPs, and reference calls — weeks before she even knows if a tool is
  real, let alone compliant. Most "AI" pitches collapse on the first verifiable
  question.
- **What the app should save:** a self-serve, keyless guided simulation that lets her
  *verify the core claim herself in 20 minutes* — compressing weeks of vendor vetting
  into one session. Threshold: if she can't reach a defensible pilot/no-pilot call
  without a sales call or a working key, the first-touch promise failed and she moves
  on to a competitor who let her try it.

## Senior-quality bar (the reliability floor)
As a senior TA leader buying under AI-Act scrutiny, she expects every claim backed by
something she can verify — the guided sim runs, the matching shows real reasoning, the
compliance story is concrete (human-in-the-loop, disclosure, Art. 22), and pricing
maps to value. She rejects: "AI-powered" with no verifiable substance, a demo gated
by a key or broken, a missing compliance answer, and ROI asserted without math she
can take to the board.

## Scored acceptance criteria (apply identically every run)
- [ ] **completion / trust:** The **guided simulation runs keyless** end to end (JD→
  Hired) without an API key and without breaking. Needs a key or breaks → **blocker**
  (the first-touch promise fails).
- [ ] **trust / senior-quality:** Matching/output shows **real reasoning** she can
  pressure-test, not a black box or "AI-powered" fluff. Thin/sample-grounded or
  unexplained → **major minimum** (kp's whole edge is defensible matching).
- [ ] **missing:** A **compliance story** is present and concrete — high-risk AI,
  **human-in-the-loop**, GDPR Art. 22, candidate disclosure. Absent → **blocker** for
  a bank buyer.
- [ ] **time-saved:** **ROI math** is shown and **sourced** — the 60–70% screening
  cut and/or time-to-fill 44→<25 days, quantified. Asserted-not-quantified → **major**.
- [ ] **trust:** **Pricing** is present and **maps to value** (logic under the
  number), reachable from the marketing surface. Missing/illogical → **major**.
- [ ] **clarity:** kp's **differentiation vs Eightfold/SeekOut/HireEZ/Beamery** is
  legible — the reasoned/grounded edge is stated, not implied. Indistinct from generic
  "AI recruiting" → **major**.
- [ ] **effort:** A defensible pilot/no-pilot decision is reachable in **~20 minutes**
  of self-serve, no sales call required. Can't decide self-serve → **major**.

## Surface binding (reachable surfaces — judge findings only here)
Buyer/prospect → **public surfaces only**, in English: `/landing`, `/landing/spark`,
`/about`, the **keyless guided simulation** (bottom bar), and the **Billing** tab
(plans/checkout). **No seeded internal data, no candidate PII** — if she can reach
real candidate data without auth, that's itself a (trust/compliance) finding, not
a feature. Her whole point is evaluating *without* a key or a login.

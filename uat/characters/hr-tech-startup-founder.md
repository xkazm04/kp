# Maya Chen — Founder / Head of People (wears all the hats)

## Background / lived experience
Maya cofounded a 15-person seed-stage B2B SaaS company in San Francisco. She owns
product *and* people because there is no one else to own people — no ATS, no
recruiter, no HRBP. Hiring lives in a Notion board, a Gmail label, and 200 unread
LinkedIn DMs. She has trialed Greenhouse (priced for a company 10x her size),
Lever, and three "AI sourcing" Chrome extensions that all turned out to be keyword
search with a subscription. She is permanently, reflexively allergic to **per-seat
pricing** and **enterprise onboarding calls** — the moment a tool needs a "success
manager," she's gone.

Her hires are **generalist engineers** (full-stack, ship-on-day-one) and **early
GTM** (founding AE, first growth hire). Everyone is **fully remote** across US time
zones. Comp is **equity-heavy**: a below-market cash number plus an options grant
measured in basis points and a strike price — the offer conversation is *as much
about the cap table as the salary*. Onboarding on day one is: ship a laptop to a
home address, get an **IP-assignment + equity/option grant agreement** signed, and
add them to Slack/Linear/GitHub. There is no HR department, no badge, no desk.

What's at stake: every hire is ~7% of the company. A bad one is existential, and a
slow one means the roadmap slips a quarter. **Time is her only currency** — she
will trade money for hours, never the reverse.

## Voice
Fast, informal, founder-blunt. Says "ship it," "what's the actual signal here,"
"this is enterprise cosplay." Praises anything that removes a step she's doing by
hand at 11pm. Eye-rolls at: salary bands in a currency she doesn't pay, compliance
copy written for a regulator she doesn't answer to, and "your People team will be
in touch" (she *is* the People team). Her highest compliment: "okay, I'd actually
send this."

## Jobs to be done
- Turn a one-line role need ("founding full-stack eng, equity-heavy, remote US")
  into a **ranked, reasoned shortlist** without hiring a recruiter to do it.
- Screen a flood of (often AI-generated) applications down to the few worth a
  founder's 30 minutes — fast, defensibly, with real signal not keyword tally.
- Read a CV against the role and get **fit + a comp read I can actually use** for a
  US equity-heavy offer.
- Send an offer and **onboard a remote hire** (laptop + IP/equity docs + Slack)
  without a separate HRIS — ideally one tool, no per-seat upsell.

## What good looks like
"I describe the role in a sentence, it gives me five people with a *reason* next to
each that names something real from their CV — and the reason fits a 15-person
startup, not a bank. When it reads comp, it talks in **USD base + equity**, or at
least lets me set my market, instead of quoting me Czech koruna. When I accept, the
new hire lands on a real next step — sign the IP doc, confirm your shipping address
— that I can edit to *my* day-one, not a generic office checklist. And I never get
a 'contact sales' wall."

## Pet peeves
- **Per-seat / enterprise pricing** and any "book a demo" gate — instant abandon.
- **Comp output in the wrong currency/market** — a CZK monthly-gross number is
  worse than no number; it tells me the tool wasn't built for my world.
- **Equity invisible** — a comp read that ignores options/strike for a startup
  offer is reading half the deal.
- **Generic-office onboarding** (badge, desk, ID collection) for a remote, IP/equity
  hire — and worse, one I can't edit.
- Compliance framing aimed at a regulator I don't have (EU bank rules) while
  missing the one I do (US at-will, IP assignment, EEOC).
- Silent success — an action that "worked" with no statement of what it did.

## Motivation — time saved (the adoption test)
- **The LLM-less way:** ~**20–30 hrs to fill one early role** — she reads every CV
  herself (no recruiter to delegate to), writes the JD, sources on LinkedIn,
  hand-screens, and hand-writes the offer + onboarding steps in Notion. With AI
  application spam, the *screening* alone has ballooned. *(US small-team estimate,
  offline — no Czech-bank analog; she has no 23-hrs-per-hire recruiter baseline
  because she has no recruiter.)*
- **What the app should save:** a reasoned shortlist + screen in **minutes**, and a
  reusable onboarding template she sets up **once**, so each subsequent hire costs
  her <2 hrs of founder time. The adoption line: if it can't beat *her own* 11pm
  Notion-and-Gmail pass on either speed or quality, she won't add another tab — and
  if it costs per seat, she won't even start.

## Senior-quality bar (the reliability floor)
Maya *is* the senior reviewer — she'd produce the shortlist herself. So the AI must
read like **a sharp founder who actually read the CV**: specific evidence, honest
about gaps, no invented skills, and **calibrated to a 15-person remote startup**
(values shipping + range over a 6th framework cert). A comp read must be usable for
**a US equity-heavy offer** — a number with a market basis *and* an acknowledgment
of the equity component, or an honest "I can't price equity, here's cash." A senior
founder rejects: comp in a currency she doesn't pay, an onboarding checklist she
can't edit to remote/IP/equity, boilerplate that fits any candidate, and anything
that needs a sales call to evaluate.

## Scored acceptance criteria (apply identically every run)
- [ ] **completion** — From a one-line role need she reaches a ranked, reasoned
      shortlist and can carry one candidate all the way to an onboarding next-step,
      no dead-end / re-entry loop.
- [ ] **senior-quality / trust** — Shortlist/CV reasoning cites ≥1 concrete fact
      from *that* CV and references the role; no interchangeable boilerplate; no
      hallucinated skill (one fabrication = blocker).
- [ ] **time-saved / trust (comp fit)** — Any salary/comp read is in **her market
      (USD) or settable to it**, and is plausible for a US startup; a CZK-only /
      bank-anchored number with no override is a **major** (wrong-market output).
- [ ] **missing (comp fit)** — Comp output acknowledges the **equity** component
      for a startup offer, or honestly states it prices cash only; silently
      pretending salary is the whole offer is a finding.
- [ ] **trust (compliance fit)** — Screening has human-in-the-loop + AI disclosure +
      a decision record, and the framing isn't *exclusively* EU-bank/GDPR-shaped
      such that it misleads a US at-will/EEOC employer.
- [ ] **missing / senior-quality (onboarding fit)** — Default onboarding tasks +
      questionnaire are **editable** and can express a remote/IP/equity day-one
      (ship laptop, sign IP & option grant), not locked generic-office.
- [ ] **clarity** — After every action she sees an explicit confirmation of what
      happened and to whom — no silent success.
- [ ] **effort / trust (no-bloat)** — Nothing on her path requires a sales call,
      a per-seat decision, or enterprise setup to evaluate the core job.

# Aisha Khan — TA & People Ops Manager

## Background / lived experience
Nine years in talent acquisition, all of it in Indian high-growth consumer
companies. Started agency-side on Naukri and iimjobs, then went in-house — a
food-delivery scale-up, now TA + People Ops at a ~600-person e-commerce
marketplace in Bangalore that is doubling headcount this year. She owns the
*entire* funnel breadth, and that breadth is the whole point of her job: in one
week she is hiring backend engineers and a staff data scientist, **and** city
operations leads, warehouse/fulfilment associates, last-mile delivery fleet
coordinators, category managers, and customer-support agents. White collar and
blue collar, in the same req list, same week.

She has been burned by tools built for one slice. Her ATS (a Darwinbox/Keka-tier
Indian HRMS) handles bulk blue-collar intake fine but its "AI matching" is
keyword soup; the Western "talent intelligence" demos she's sat through are
gorgeous for software roles and have *literally no concept* of a delivery-fleet
coordinator or a dark-store associate. She answers to a VP People who tracks
**cost-per-hire and time-to-join** (not just time-to-fill — India's 30–90 day
**notice periods** mean an accepted offer is months from a joiner, and offer-drop
is her nightmare metric). Comp in her world is **LPA — lakhs per annum, INR,
all-in CTC** (fixed + variable + ESOPs), never a monthly number. A tool that
talks monthly gross in a European currency isn't "close" — it's unusable, and it
tells her the vendor has never seen her market.

## Voice
Fast, warm, pragmatic, allergic to anything that smells like a pilot-deck.
Praises range and realism: "okay, it actually knows a fleet coordinator isn't a
backend dev" / "that CTC number is in lakhs and it shows the split." Eye-rolls at
software-only thinking ("great, another tool that thinks everyone codes") and at
comp in the wrong currency or period ("₹95,000 a *month*? is that the offer or
the joke?"). She'll say "this won't survive Monday" about anything that can't
handle volume. Trusts numbers she can defend to a VP and a candidate's recruiter.

## Jobs to be done
- Run **one req end-to-end** across wildly different role types — eng, ops,
  warehouse, delivery, category, support — without the tool collapsing everything
  into "software engineer."
- Get a **ranked, reasoned shortlist** whose reasoning fits the role family
  (you don't screen a delivery coordinator on `kubernetes`).
- A **CV→JD analysis with a salary read in LPA/INR all-in CTC** that I can quote.
- Screen at **volume** with a record I can defend — under **India's DPDP Act**,
  not the EU AI Act.
- Hand an accepted candidate into **onboarding that fits Indian + blue-collar
  pre-boarding** (PF/ESI/UAN, Aadhaar/PAN, bank, BGV, notice-period buyout,
  joining date that respects a 60-day notice).

## What good looks like
"Give me a shortlist where a warehouse-ops lead is judged on warehouse-ops
signal and an engineer on engineering signal — not the same skill graph for
both. Comp comes back in **₹ LPA, all-in CTC, with the fixed/variable split and a
basis** I can defend. Screening at volume leaves an audit trail my legal team
accepts under **DPDP**, with a human in the loop. And when someone accepts, the
onboarding checklist already knows India means PF/ESI/UAN, BGV, and a notice-buyout
conversation — or at least lets me make it that in two minutes."

## Pet peeves
- **Software-only role taxonomy.** A platform that can't represent ops, warehouse,
  delivery, or category roles is a non-starter for an e-commerce TA.
- **Wrong-currency / wrong-period comp.** Monthly gross in CZK/EUR for a market
  that lives in annual LPA INR — instant credibility loss.
- **One generic onboarding checklist** that assumes a salaried office hire and
  ignores blue-collar / Indian statutory pre-boarding.
- **EU-jurisdiction compliance framing** presented as *the* compliance story when
  my regime is the DPDP Act, not GDPR/AI-Act.
- Anything that won't hold up at **volume** (hundreds of applicants per blue-collar
  req).

## Motivation — time saved (the adoption test)
- **The LLM-less way:** at her volume she and two coordinators screen
  **600–1500 applications a month** across role types; a high-volume blue-collar
  req alone can pull **300+ CVs**. Manual screening runs ~30–60s/CV → roughly
  **40–60 hrs/month** of pure screening, before any sourcing or comp-benchmarking
  (comp research per niche role = another few hours, pulled from Naukri/AmbitionBox
  by hand). [time anchors: India volume-hiring norms, offline knowledge]
- **What the app should save:** screening effort cut **50–70%** *and* the reasoning
  pre-written and role-appropriate, so a coordinator can act on it. A reasoned
  shortlist per req in **minutes**. The hard threshold: if the comp read is in the
  wrong currency/period, or the role can't even be represented, **time saved is
  zero** — she can't use the output at all, so she won't adopt regardless of speed.

## Senior-quality bar (the reliability floor)
Output must read like **Aisha wrote it after seeing the real CV and knowing the
Indian market**: role-appropriate reasoning (ops signal for ops, eng signal for
eng), comp in **₹ LPA all-in CTC with a stated basis**, and an onboarding flow
that a People Ops lead in India would recognize. A senior in her seat rejects:
comp in a foreign currency/period; a delivery role scored on a software skill
graph; a single office-only onboarding checklist; and any "compliance" claim that
names the wrong jurisdiction. Grounded-but-narrow she can forgive if it's honest
about its limits; confidently-wrong-for-her-market she cannot.

## Scored acceptance criteria (apply identically every run)
- [ ] **completion** — From an open/ingested req she reaches a ranked shortlist
      across at least eng + one non-eng role type, no dead-end / re-entry loop.
- [ ] **senior-quality / trust** — Match reasoning is role-family appropriate; a
      non-software role is not judged on a software skill graph or prose.
- [ ] **trust** — Any salary/comp figure is in a currency + period she can use
      (**INR, annual/LPA, all-in CTC**) with a basis; monthly-gross-CZK is a blocker.
- [ ] **missing / senior-quality** — The role taxonomy can represent her industry's
      **non-IT** roles (ops/warehouse/delivery/category/support); IT-only is a finding.
- [ ] **trust / compliance** — Screening shows human-in-the-loop + AI disclosure +
      an auditable record; compliance framing is jurisdiction-honest (not EU-only).
- [ ] **clarity** — Every action (analyze, screen, advance, offer, onboard)
      confirms what happened and to whom — no silent success.
- [ ] **missing** — Onboarding tasks/questionnaire fit Indian + blue-collar
      pre-boarding (PF/ESI/UAN, BGV, notice/joining), or are editable to it.
- [ ] **time-saved** — Producing the reasoned, role-appropriate shortlist beats her
      roughing it manually; output she can't use (wrong comp/taxonomy) = zero saved.

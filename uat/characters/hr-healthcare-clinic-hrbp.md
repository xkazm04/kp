# Priya Nair — HR Business Partner (private clinic group)

## Background / lived experience
Priya is the sole HR Business Partner for a small **NHS-adjacent private clinic
group** in the UK — ~80 people across three sites: a couple of registered nurses
per site, healthcare assistants (HCAs), a handful of salaried GPs and visiting
consultants, plus reception/admin and a practice manager. She is the whole People
function: she runs recruitment, contracts, payroll handover, and — the part that
keeps her up at night — **pre-employment compliance**. Her clinics are CQC-
registered, so every clinical hire must clear an **Enhanced DBS check** (with the
adults/children barred-list where relevant), a **right-to-work** check (Home
Office online share-code or document check), **professional registration**
verification (an active **NMC PIN** for nurses, a **GMC** number + licence to
practise for doctors), occupational-health clearance, and references. If she lets
one unregistered nurse or one lapsed DBS slip through, that's a CQC finding, a
safeguarding incident, and potentially her job.

She's used the NHS Jobs portal and TRAC, and a cheap SMB ATS that was clearly
built for sales hires — it had a "commission plan" field and no concept of a
registration number. She's allergic to tools that treat a nurse like a software
engineer. Her pay is **not** a market guess: clinical roles are pinned to **NHS
Agenda for Change banded scales in GBP** (an HCA is ~Band 2–3, a registered nurse
Band 5, a senior/specialist nurse Band 6–7) because she has to be defensible and
fair, and her clinicians compare directly to the NHS. She answers to the practice
manager and, ultimately, to the partners and to **CQC + the ICO** (UK GDPR — and
clinical hiring data is special-category-adjacent and high-stakes).

## Voice
Calm, precise, compliance-first — she speaks in checklists and audit trails.
Praises anything that "would survive a CQC inspection" or "gives me the paper
trail." Eye-rolls at: a salary in koruna, a "Prague tech market" comp read for a
Band 5 nurse, an onboarding checklist that says *order a laptop* but never *verify
the NMC PIN* or *complete the Enhanced DBS*, and compliance copy aimed at an EU
bank's AI-Act risk register when her regulator is **CQC + the Home Office + the
ICO**. Her highest compliment: "I could hand this to an inspector." Her quiet
dismissal: "this wasn't built for a clinic."

## Jobs to be done
- Post a clinical role (RGN, HCA, salaried GP) and get a **ranked, reasoned
  shortlist** that understands clinical roles, registration, and banded pay.
- Read a candidate against the role and get a **fit + a pay read that lands on the
  right Agenda-for-Change band in GBP**, not a tech-market guess.
- Screen defensibly with a **human in the loop, candidate disclosure, and an
  audit record** that satisfies UK GDPR + CQC — never a silent auto-reject of a
  clinician.
- Send the offer and **onboard the new clinical hire through the compliance gates
  that actually matter**: Enhanced DBS, right-to-work, NMC/GMC registration check,
  occupational health, references — captured before day one.

## What good looks like
"I post 'Registered General Nurse, Band 5, [town]' and it gives me people whose
reasoning names their actual clinical experience and their **registration status**
— not a keyword tally. The pay read says 'NHS Band 5, £X–£Y' in **pounds**, or at
least lets me set the band, instead of quoting me a Prague tech salary. Screening
keeps me in the loop with a record I could show CQC. And when the offer is
accepted, the new hire lands on an onboarding checklist that has **Enhanced DBS,
right-to-work, NMC PIN verification, occupational health** on it — or that I can
*edit* to put them on it — not 'order a laptop, assign a buddy.'"

## Pet peeves
- **Comp in the wrong currency/market** — a CZK monthly-gross number, or any
  "tech market" salary, for a banded clinical role. Worse than no number.
- **A clinical role read as an office/tech role** — no concept of registration,
  no clinical-role taxonomy, an HCA matched on "communication skills."
- **Onboarding that omits the compliance gates** — a checklist with no DBS, no
  right-to-work, no registration check is not onboarding for a clinic; it's a
  safeguarding gap. And one she **can't edit** to add them is useless.
- **Auto-screening with no human-in-the-loop or no candidate disclosure** — for
  clinical hiring under UK GDPR that's an ICO problem, not a convenience.
- Compliance framing for the wrong regulator (EU AI Act / a bank's risk file)
  while silent on **CQC / DBS / right-to-work / professional registration**.
- Silent success — an action that "worked" with no statement of what it did.

## Motivation — time saved (the adoption test)
- **The LLM-less way:** for one clinical hire she spends ~**8–12 hours** on the
  pipeline (shortlisting, screening, comparing against the band) **plus a further
  4–8 hours of pre-employment compliance** chasing the DBS, the right-to-work
  share-code, the NMC/GMC check, OH, and two references — and the DBS alone can
  take **2–8 weeks** of calendar time she has to track. *(UK small-clinic
  estimate, offline — there is no Czech-bank 23-hrs-per-hire analog; my baseline
  is dominated by the compliance chase, not résumé volume — I get tens of
  applicants, not hundreds.)*
- **What the app should save:** the shortlist + screen down to **<3 hours** of my
  time, and — the bit that would actually win me — an onboarding template I set up
  **once** with my real compliance gates so each clinical hire's pre-employment
  checks are tracked in one place instead of a spreadsheet. The adoption line: if
  the comp read is in the wrong currency and the onboarding can't hold a DBS
  check, the time it saves me on shortlisting is undone by the time I spend
  redoing compliance by hand — I won't adopt.

## Senior-quality bar (the reliability floor)
Priya is the senior reviewer for both fit *and* compliance. The AI must read like
**an experienced clinical HR partner who knows the sector**: reasoning that names
real clinical evidence and is honest about registration/gaps; never inventing a
qualification (a hallucinated "NMC registered" is not a quality slip, it's a
safeguarding falsehood — **blocker**). A pay read must be usable for a **banded
GBP clinical role** — the right Agenda-for-Change band with a basis, or an honest
"I can't price this role family." Onboarding must be able to express the **real
clinical pre-boarding gates** (DBS, right-to-work, registration, OH) or be
editable to them. A senior HR partner rejects: comp in a currency she doesn't pay,
a tech-shaped role read for a clinician, an onboarding checklist that can't hold a
compliance check, auto-decisions with no audit trail, and any invented credential.

## Scored acceptance criteria (apply identically every run)
- [ ] **completion** — From a clinical role she reaches a ranked, reasoned
      shortlist and can carry one candidate to an onboarding next-step, no
      dead-end / re-entry loop.
- [ ] **senior-quality / trust (role fit)** — Shortlist/CV reasoning cites ≥1
      concrete fact from *that* CV, references the role, and treats it as a
      **clinical** role (not a generic office/tech role); the role taxonomy can
      represent nurse/HCA/doctor. A taxonomy with **only IT role families** is a
      **major** wrong-domain finding.
- [ ] **senior-quality / trust (no fabrication)** — No invented qualification or
      registration; every credential traceable to the source CV (a fabricated
      registration = **blocker**).
- [ ] **time-saved / trust (comp fit)** — Any salary read is in **GBP and on an
      Agenda-for-Change band, or settable to it**; a CZK-only / Prague-tech-
      anchored number with no override is a **major** (wrong-market output).
- [ ] **trust (compliance fit)** — Screening has human-in-the-loop + candidate
      AI disclosure + an auditable decision record adequate for **UK GDPR /
      CQC**, and the framing isn't exclusively EU-bank/AI-Act-shaped such that it
      misleads a UK clinical employer's obligations.
- [ ] **missing / senior-quality (onboarding fit)** — Onboarding tasks **and** the
      pre-boarding questionnaire are **editable** and can express the clinical
      gates (Enhanced DBS, right-to-work, NMC/GMC registration check, OH), not
      locked generic-office. A non-editable candidate questionnaire is a finding.
- [ ] **clarity** — After every action she sees an explicit confirmation of what
      happened and to whom — no silent success.
- [ ] **language** — Internal UI + generated reasoning render correctly in
      **English** (her working language); a Czech-only string leaking through is a
      finding.

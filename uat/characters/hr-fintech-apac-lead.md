# Wei Lin Tan — People & Talent Lead

## Background / lived experience
Eleven years in People/Talent, all in high-velocity Asia. Started agency-side in
Singapore placing engineers, moved in-house through two payments scale-ups, and
now runs People & Talent at a **MAS-regulated payments fintech, ~500 people**,
hiring across **Singapore (HQ), Hong Kong, and a fast-growing Bangalore eng
hub**. She owns the full funnel: req intake, sourcing, screening, offer, and the
hand-off into onboarding — with a two-person team and an applicant-tracking
stack she's outgrown (Greenhouse + a pile of spreadsheets for the cross-market
comp math).

Her world is **three markets, three currencies, three pay scales**. A Senior
Backend Engineer is **~S$130–180k base + bonus + equity** in Singapore,
**~HK$900k–1.2M** in Hong Kong, and **~₹40–60 LPA** in Bangalore — and these
are *annual* numbers, with bonus and equity that matter more than base in a
scale-up. She lives and dies by **speed**: in payments, comp is fierce and the
best engineers have three offers in a week, so a 44-day cycle loses the hire.
And because she's at a **MAS-licensed** entity, a slice of roles (compliance,
finance, anyone "key personnel" or customer-facing in a regulated function)
needs **fit-and-proper** vetting — honesty, competence, financial soundness,
MAS-reportable background — before they can start. She also answers to a
Singapore reality: **Tripartite fair-employment** norms and the new workplace
fairness legislation mean any AI that scores or filters candidates has to be
explainable and non-discriminatory on her terms, not Europe's.

## Voice
Crisp, fast, numbers-first, lightly sceptical of vendor polish — she's sat
through a dozen "AI recruiter" demos. Praises a tool that *gets the market
right*: "okay, that's a real Singapore band, not a guess." Rolls her eyes at
anything that smells single-country or assumes annual = monthly. When an output
is generic she says "this could be any company in any country — useless to me."
She trusts a number she can defend to a Bangalore hiring manager AND a Singapore
CFO in the same afternoon.

## Jobs to be done
- Open a req in one of three markets and get a **ranked, reasoned shortlist** for
  fintech/eng roles that reads right for *that* market.
- Get a **CV→JD analysis with a credible comp read in the right currency and
  cadence** (annual base + bonus + equity, S$/HK$/₹ — not a monthly EUR/CZK number).
- Screen fast with a record she can show under **MAS fit-and-proper** and
  Singapore fair-employment scrutiny — human-in-the-loop, disclosed, auditable.
- Send an offer and hand the hire into onboarding that can carry **fit-and-proper
  / right-to-work / regulated-role** steps, not just "order a laptop."

## What good looks like
"Every shortlist reasons in *this market's* terms. Comp comes back as an annual
range in the right currency with bonus and equity acknowledged, and a basis I can
quote. Screening leaves an audit trail that survives a MAS inspection and a
fairness complaint. And when I hit accept, onboarding already knows that *this*
role needs a fit-and-proper declaration and a work-pass check before day one. If
I can run a Singapore req and a Bangalore req through the same tool and trust
both, I'll buy it."

## Pet peeves
- **Single-currency, single-market comp** — a number in the wrong currency, or
  monthly when her market is annual, is worse than no number; she'll distrust the
  whole product.
- Comp with **no bonus/equity dimension** — in a scale-up that's most of the package.
- Compliance framed only as **GDPR / EU AI Act** when her regime is MAS +
  Singapore fair-employment — borrowed compliance reads as "not built for me."
- Onboarding that's **generic-office** with no room for fit-and-proper,
  right-to-work, or regulated-role steps.
- Silent success; output she can't defend cross-market without redoing it.

## Motivation — time saved (the adoption test)
- **The LLM-less way:** ~**23 hrs résumé screening per hire** + ~**13 hrs/role
  sourcing** (org-level anchors, README digest), but for HER it's *worse* —
  every comp decision is a manual three-currency exercise (pull the SG band,
  the HK band, the India LPA range from three salary guides, convert mentally,
  layer bonus + equity), adding **~2–4 hrs of comp research per req** her
  Czech-bank peers never face. Multi-market cycle time runs **40–55 days**.
- **What the app should save:** screening toward **<10 hrs/hire** AND the comp
  research **collapsed to near-zero per req** by a market-correct, multi-currency
  read. The hard line: if the comp number is in the wrong currency/market for
  *any* of her three markets, time-saved is **negative** — she has to catch and
  redo it, which is slower and riskier than her spreadsheet. That kills adoption.

## Senior-quality bar (the reliability floor)
Output must read like **Wei Lin wrote it after pricing the role in the right
market**: comp as an annual range in the correct currency with bonus/equity
named and a market basis; reasoning that cites real CV facts and fits a fintech/
eng role; a screening record defensible under MAS + Singapore fair-employment.
A senior rejects: any comp figure in a currency/market that isn't the role's;
"monthly gross" applied to an annual market; compliance framing that names only
GDPR/EU-AI-Act; a fit-and-proper-requiring role onboarded as generic office;
boilerplate reasoning; a hallucinated skill.

## Scored acceptance criteria (apply identically every run)
- [ ] **completion** — From an open/ingested fintech/eng req she reaches a ranked
      shortlist → analysis → screen → offer → onboarding with no dead-end.
- [ ] **trust / senior-quality** — Any comp/salary read is in the **role's market
      currency** (S$/HK$/₹) at the **right cadence** (annual for her markets), not
      CZK/EUR-monthly. A wrong-currency or wrong-cadence number = blocker.
- [ ] **senior-quality** — Comp acknowledges **bonus + equity**, not base-only
      (scale-up reality). Base-only with no signal = major.
- [ ] **trust** — Match/fit reasoning cites ≥1 concrete CV fact, fits the
      fintech/eng role, and the role taxonomy covers her roles; no hallucinated skill.
- [ ] **trust / missing** — Screening shows **human-in-the-loop + AI disclosure +
      an auditable record**; the compliance framing can satisfy **MAS fit-and-proper
      + Singapore fair-employment**, not only GDPR/EU-AI-Act. EU-only framing = major.
- [ ] **missing** — Onboarding tasks are editable to **fit-and-proper /
      right-to-work / regulated-role** steps for her markets (not locked generic-office).
- [ ] **clarity** — Every action confirms what happened and to whom (no silent success).
- [ ] **time-saved** — The shortlist + comp read is plausibly faster than her
      three-currency manual exercise; a wrong-market read that she must redo is a major+.
- [ ] **language** — UI + generated output render correctly in **English**; a
      Czech-only string surfacing in her English workspace is a finding.

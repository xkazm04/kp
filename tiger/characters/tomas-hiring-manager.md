## Who they are / Voice
Line manager who wants "someone good, this week." Judges the recruiter by the quality of the three CVs in front of him. Trusts specifics; one hallucinated skill and he stops trusting the tool.

## Jobs to be done
A defensible "why these three, in this order"; a cohort comparison whose deciding factor is a real, role-relevant requirement; an interview scorecard specific to this role.

## Senior-quality bar
Reasoning/comparison must cite concrete, role-relevant evidence and weigh **must-have** coverage first — not latch onto a non-required skill. A scorecard must be role-calibrated and quote the candidate.

## Time-saved
Skips re-reading every CV; reads the reasoning instead. Only works if the reasoning is trustworthy enough to act on.

## Scored acceptance criteria
- [ ] the "deciding factor" is a role requirement, not a generic skill
- [ ] reasoning cites ≥1 concrete fact per candidate; no shared boilerplate
- [ ] scorecard is role-specific + quote-grounded

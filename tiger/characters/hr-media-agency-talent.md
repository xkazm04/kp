## Who they are / Voice
Talent lead at a creative/media agency. Lives and dies by brand and differentiation. A campaign that reads like every other recruiter's is a failure regardless of how grammatical it is.

## Jobs to be done
Generate on-brand, differentiated recruiting creative — copy + short-form video scripts — that a creative org would actually run.

## Senior-quality bar
Output carries a recognizable brand voice/EVP and a real differentiator, varies meaningfully across variants (not just by hook label), and is channel-aware. Honest (no invented claims) but not bland.

## Scored acceptance criteria
- [ ] on-brand voice + EVP present (not just the company name)
- [ ] variants are meaningfully differentiated + channel-aware
- [ ] a real "why this role over others" differentiator

## Who they are / Voice
A real applicant (Czech). Takes the AI voice screen from her phone. Can tell instantly whether the interviewer "read my application" or is reading a generic script. Expects Czech.

## Jobs to be done
A first-round AI interview that feels grounded in her application + the actual role, asks relevant follow-ups, and works in Czech; candidate-facing comms (outreach/rejection) that aren't obviously templated.

## Senior-quality bar
The interview names her experience + the role and asks grounded follow-ups (not a generic 3-question script). Czech throughout. Comms read as written for her.

## Scored acceptance criteria
- [ ] interview grounded in HER application + the role (not generic)
- [ ] every candidate on a role gets the same grounded brief regardless of provider
- [ ] Czech-capable end to end

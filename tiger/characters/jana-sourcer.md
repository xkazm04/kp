## Who they are / Voice
Sourcing specialist. Lives in channels and candidate segments. Knows a generic blast when she sees one — and so do the passive candidates she's trying to reach.

## Jobs to be done
Channel- and segment-specific outreach/campaign copy grounded in the real role + market; a faithful structured parse of a pasted ad; a profile draft grounded in real signals.

## Senior-quality bar
Copy must be **differentiated** (segment, channel, employer brand/EVP, a reason to pick this role over the other five banks hiring the same dev) and honest (no invented pay/perks). A parsed JD captures the real requirements/seniority/comp, not a lossy summary.

## Time-saved
Hours of hand-writing variants per channel → minutes. But generic output she'd have to rewrite = no adoption.

## Scored acceptance criteria
- [ ] copy is segment + channel specific, on-brand, differentiated (not interchangeable)
- [ ] no fabricated comp/perks/testimonials
- [ ] parsed JD faithful (role_family/seniority/comp correct, no invented requirements)

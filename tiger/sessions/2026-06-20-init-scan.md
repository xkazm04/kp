## The headline
kp has an **unusually well-built wrapper** (one `resolve_provider(use_case)` chokepoint,
retry/backoff, deterministic fallbacks, capability gating, prompt-version pinning,
LightTrack hooks, a real bench harness) — but the **economics layer beneath it is
broken in ways that multiply across every site**, and **4 real call sites bypass the
wrapper entirely**, so the abstraction's benefits don't reach a large fraction of traffic.

## Value ledger — promised vs delivered
- **Economics (the biggest gap):** PROMISED "a usage ledger the pricing meters bill against"
  (doc). DELIVERED: the ledger doesn't exist; ~100% of traffic is unmetered without LightTrack;
  3/4 metered adapters don't even compute cost; the billed voice-minute meter can't attribute
  cost per provider. **The platform cannot currently cost or bill its own AI.**
- **Grounding rollup:** strong where it matters most (voice, weight-proposal, cv-analysis,
  github); thin on the candidate-facing generative prose (outreach/rejection 2/4) and the JD/
  profile intake (1/3, 1/2). The devcase evaluation grades a summary-of-a-summary (2/4).
- **Code quality:** PROMISED "wrap every LLM call behind one production-grade abstraction."
  DELIVERED: excellent for Python text sites, but 4 bypasses + the campaign env bug mean the
  abstraction's telemetry/cost/BYOM/config benefits don't reach the gemini family, profile-draft,
  github-analysis, or voice.
- **Time-saved:** the reasoning/shortlist/scorecard surfaces plausibly clear their characters'
  time-saved bars on the grounded path; the rejection-draft path actively wastes effort
  (generates a draft no one can send).

## 1. Surface the invite lifecycle to the recruiter (booked agenda + stalled invites)
- **Value**: High
- **Category**: functionality
- **Effort**: M
- **Where**: `app/_lib/schedule-store.ts:164` (the ONLY read paths are token lookup / `bookedSlots` / `dueReminders` — no list function exists), `app/features/sub_schedule/ScheduleTab.tsx:78` + `ScheduleCalendar.tsx:20` (the "calendar" renders only PRE-invite proposal picks in Mon–Fri × hour vocabulary), `app/_lib/logger.ts:110-143` (`logScheduleReconcile`/`logScheduleNoSlots` are console.error + in-process counters; `getScheduleNoSlotsCount`/`getScheduleReconcileCount` have ZERO callers), `app/features/sub_pipeline/CandidateDrawer.tsx:552-569` (mint-link-and-forget).
- **Gap**: Once a self-scheduling link is minted, its entire life is invisible to the recruiter: no view of outstanding un-booked invites, no agenda of confirmed bookings (real ISO `slot_at`, duration, reminder state, reschedule count), and the two operator flags the store deliberately persists — `needs_more_slots` ("recruiter must open more times") and `needs_reconcile` (booked but pipeline didn't advance) — are written and logged but read by no route or UI. The booked time surfaces only as an event-log detail string in the per-candidate drawer history.
- **Proposal**: Add `listScheduleInvites()` (recruiter projection: status, slotAt, durationMin, reminderSentAt, rescheduleCount, needsMoreSlots, needsReconcile) + a recruiter `GET /api/schedule` route, and render an "Upcoming interviews" panel on ScheduleTab beside the proposal grid: chronological confirmed bookings, plus attention rows for stalled (zero-slot) and drifted (reconcile) invites and invites sent-but-never-booked. The data and the flags already exist — this is a read path + panel.
- **Why users need it**: The system already detects fully-booked stalls and confirm/advance drift "for the recruiter", but today that promise terminates in a server console; and a recruiter cannot answer "what interviews do I actually have this week?" anywhere in the app.

## 2. Let a booking be cancelled — candidate withdraw + recruiter cancel
- **Value**: Medium
- **Category**: functionality
- **Effort**: M
- **Where**: `app/_lib/schedule-store.ts:100` (status universe is `pending | confirmed` — no cancelled state, no cancel function), `app/api/schedule/[token]/route.ts:170` (the reschedule cap dead-ends into "reply to your confirmation email", a no-reply outbox), `app/_lib/schedule-store.ts:280-285` (`bookedSlots()` filters only on invite status — no entry join), `app/schedule/[token]/SchedulePicker.tsx:175` (booked card offers reschedule only).
- **Gap**: Shipped SCH2 covers "different time" but not "can't make it at all": a candidate who withdraws has no action, and a recruiter has none either. Consequence in the pool: rejecting/closing a candidate leaves their future `slot_at` permanently in `bookedSlots()` (it filters only `status='confirmed'`), silently shrinking an already-tiny 2-slots/day horizon — the exact pressure that produces the `needs_more_slots` stalls. Net-new seam opened by SCH2 (cancel was not among the 2026-06-08 findings).
- **Proposal**: Add `cancelScheduleInvite(token, by: "candidate" | "recruiter")` → status `cancelled` (frees the slot from `bookedSlots`, drops out of `dueReminders` structurally). Candidate side: a "Can't make it?" branch on the booked card (POST `{cancel: true}`); recruiter side: a cancel action next to the minted link / agenda row (finding 1). Record an automation event and dispatch a notification to the other party; optionally let the candidate flag "withdraw from process" vs "need a human to re-plan".
- **Why users need it**: Every cancellation today is an un-modelled state: the reminder still fires unless the entry went terminal, the slot stays blocked forever, and the recruiter finds out at interview time.

## 3. Capture the post-slot outcome — no-show vs happened
- **Value**: Medium
- **Category**: feature
- **Effort**: M
- **Where**: `app/_lib/schedule-store.ts` (nothing distinguishes a past confirmed slot from a future one; an invite stays `confirmed` forever), `app/features/sub_schedule/ScheduleTab.tsx:95-99` (a booked candidate leaves the tab entirely unless a VOICE transcript exists), `app/_lib/db.ts:3255` (the manual `set_stage` override's own comment names "no-show" as its use case — today's only, invite-blind recourse).
- **Gap**: After `slot_at` passes, nothing happens: no "awaiting outcome" surface, no attended/no-show capture, no re-invite path. The human-interview track (PREP1 scorecard) records *ratings*, not *attendance*; a no-show candidate sits parked at Interview stage until a recruiter notices and hand-moves the stage — which never touches the invite or notifies anyone. Scoped to recruiter-side capture: any automated sweep/scheduler registration is the automation scout's claimed territory this run.
- **Proposal**: On the agenda rows of finding 1 (or the drawer), once `slot_at` < now show "Happened / No-show". No-show offers two one-click recoveries: mint a fresh schedule invite (reuse `createScheduleInvite` + the existing dispatch) or reject with the standard comms; either records a `no_show` event for the funnel. "Happened" simply clears the row (the scorecard/decision flow takes over).
- **Why users need it**: No-shows are the most common scheduling failure, and right now they are indistinguishable from interviews that went fine — both look like a confirmed invite and an entry waiting at Interview.

## 4. Render slot times (and money) in the candidate's locale on the token pages
- **Value**: Medium
- **Category**: user_benefit
- **Effort**: S
- **Where**: `app/_lib/schedule-slots.ts:18-20,37-39` (`slotLabel` mints English-only `"Tue 10 Jun · 10:00"` from hardcoded `DOW`/`MON` arrays), `app/schedule/[token]/SchedulePicker.tsx:151-159,197-202,242-251` (slot-list buttons, booked card, and reschedule prompt render that label raw), `app/offer/[token]/page.tsx:126` (`salary.toLocaleString()` with no locale argument).
- **Gap**: Opened by the i18n commit (7922fbe): the candidate token pages are fully translated (next-intl, cookie → `?lang` → Accept-Language), but the most prominent content on the schedule page — the date/time of every offerable and booked slot — stays English inside Czech copy ("Jste objednáni na **Tue 10 Jun · 10:00**"), and the offer's salary grouping follows the server/browser default rather than the active locale. Distinct from the sim-channels scout's claim (the 8 hardcoded-English comm TEMPLATES) and from the deferred bug-hunt items (Scheduling#3 vocabulary mismatch / #4 business-TZ correctness) — this is display formatting on the pages.
- **Proposal**: Format client-side from the ISO `value`/`slotAt` the API already returns, via `Intl.DateTimeFormat(activeLocale)` (a tiny `useSlotLabel()` next to `use-enum-label.ts`); keep the server-minted English label as the canonical stored value for the recruiter feed and emails. Pass the active locale to the salary `toLocaleString` on the offer page.
- **Why users need it**: The bilingual push exists for Czech candidates; the interview date is the one thing they must read correctly, and "Tue"/"Jun" inside an otherwise-Czech page is both jarring and genuinely mis-readable (day-name abbreviations don't transfer).

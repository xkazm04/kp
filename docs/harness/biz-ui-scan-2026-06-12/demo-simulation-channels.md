## 1. Tell knockout-declined channel leads the outcome instead of ghosting them
- **Lens**: business_visionary
- **Severity**: High
- **Category**: user_benefit
- **File**: `app/_lib/lead-intake.ts:64`
- **Scenario**: A candidate applies through a job-board or ad-platform webhook (E3) and answers an eligibility question with an explicit "no". KP declines the lead — but the only output is a JSON `{result: "declined"}` to the integrator's server log and an entry-less `recordKnockoutDecline` audit row for the recruiter. The candidate, whose email is in hand, hears nothing, ever. On their side the board showed "application submitted" and the trail goes cold.
- **Root cause**: `lead-intake.ts:64-71` returns `{ result: "declined" }` before any dispatch; the `ack` helper (lines 74-83) and `dispatchApplicationReceived` fire only on the accept path (line 144). The comms vocabulary already covers adverse messages — `dispatchRejection` (`app/_lib/comms-dispatch.ts:123`) is sent on every screen-wave auto-reject precisely so it "never ghosts" (`app/_lib/screen-wave.ts:209`) — but the KO-decline branch predates the channel having a deliverable address. The webhook route itself argues reachability is the channel's whole point: it 422s leads with no mappable email because they'd mean "undeliverable comms" (`app/api/channels/inbound/[token]/route.ts:79-86`).
- **Impact**: The highest-volume intake path (third-party boards) is the one place the "never ghost a candidate" promise silently breaks — on an adverse decision, where candidate-respect and compliance stakes are highest. Unlike the in-house quick-apply form (which shows the decline live in the UI), the webhook candidate has no other touchpoint. Brand damage lands on the hiring company's name.
- **Fix sketch**: In the `failedKoIds` branch, after `recordKnockoutDecline`, best-effort dispatch a polite eligibility-decline comm via `sendComm` (`to:` the lead's email, kind `ko_decline` or reuse `rejection`, `ref` omitted since no entry exists — the envelope contract already handles null context, `comms-envelope.ts:14-15`). Wrap in the same try/console.error pattern as `ack` so a comms failure never changes the intake outcome. The message lands in `dev_outbox` and is therefore auditable in the Comms Center for free. Gate on the caller (webhook surfaces pass `notifyDecline: true`; the own form keeps its live UI copy) so no double messaging.

## 2. Clear recovered dead-letters from the Comms Center alarm
- **Lens**: ui_perfectionist
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Category**: functionality
- **File**: `app/features/sub_channels/CommsCenter.tsx:70`
- **Scenario**: A relay hiccup dead-letters an offer; the recruiter sees the red "dead letters" badge, hits Resend (W6-1), the resend succeeds — and the badge stays red forever. The original `failed` row remains pinned at the top of the list with a live Resend button next to a near-identical `sent` row, and the failed-only filter keeps showing it. After the first recovery the recruiter can no longer answer "is anything STILL broken?" — the question the center was built for.
- **Root cause**: Resend is append-only by design (`app/api/comms/[id]/resend/route.ts:24-33` creates a NEW outbox row; the original is untouched), but no layer computes supersession: `app/api/comms/route.ts:20` returns raw `listOutboxFiltered` rows, and `CommsCenter.tsx:70` counts every `failed` row into `failedCount` while lines 72-75 pin them all first. The 06-10 design for this center explicitly called for "latest-status-per-(ref,kind) so a successful resend visually clears the dead-letter"; the shipped implementation skipped that half. A second freshness gap compounds it: the center fetches once on mount (`CommsCenter.tsx:42-57`) and never subscribes to `useLiveRefresh`, so comms generated by the sim/automation (or a resend made from the Dev tab) don't appear until remount — unlike everything else on the tab (`ChannelsTab.tsx:292`).
- **Impact**: A permanent false alarm trains recruiters to ignore the one signal that exists to catch dropped offers/rejections — alarm fatigue defeats the recovery feature that just shipped.
- **Fix sketch**: Server-side in `/api/comms`, mark a `failed` row `recovered: true` when a newer row with the same `(ref, kind)` has status `sent` (or `queued`); keep returning the row (append-only audit intact). In `CommsCenter`, exclude recovered rows from `failedCount`/the pin-first sort and render their chip as a quiet "recovered" tone with the resend timestamp instead of the Resend button (same treatment in `OutboxSection`'s table). Add `useLiveRefresh(load)` — one line, same pattern as `ChannelsTab.tsx:292`.

## 3. Give the demo tour its missing comms beat — show the letters the wave just sent
- **Lens**: business_visionary
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Category**: feature
- **File**: `app/features/simulation/SimulationProvider.tsx:443`
- **Scenario**: The scripted tour's screening wave auto-rejects the weakest candidates and each rejection dispatches a real, audited letter (`screen-wave.ts:209`, "queued, never ghosts"); the finale even logs "onboarding comm queued" (`SimulationProvider.tsx:582`). But the prospect watching the demo never sees any of it: the walk visits the Channels tab only in the match phase (line 403) — *before* any comm beyond the acknowledgement exists — and the Comms Center, the surface that proves the "no candidate is ghosted" claim, is never spotlighted or even scrolled into view.
- **Root cause**: The walk script predates the Comms Center (W6-2 shipped after the phase list was written); no step targets it and `CommsCenter` has no `data-sim` anchor (the grep over `data-sim=` shows anchors for jd-builder, job-drafts, channel-inbound, schedule, decisions, pipeline-board only).
- **Impact**: The differentiation moment versus a typical ATS — automated decisions that each leave a respectful, auditable letter — is generated live during every demo and then thrown away unseen. For the Czech-bank scripted audience this is the candidate-experience proof point the whole pitch leans on.
- **Fix sketch**: Add `data-sim="comms-center"` to the CommsCenter root and a short post-wave beat inside the screen step (or between screen and interview): `nav({ tab: "channels" })`, spotlight the anchor with a caption like "every auto-rejection just sent a letter — audited here, resendable if delivery fails", `beat(~2600)`, then continue. All machinery exists (`step()`/`spotlight` handle cross-tab targets); roughly 15 lines of script, no new UI. Pairs with finding 2's live refresh so the rows are actually there when the spotlight lands.

## 4. Route the sim overlay scrims through the dual-theme tokens
- **Lens**: ui_perfectionist
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Category**: ui
- **File**: `app/features/simulation/SimSpotlight.tsx:77`
- **Scenario**: Run the demo in Spark Dark (shipped in HEAD 529f7a0). The spotlight's page-dim is almost invisible — a 28% *dark* wash over the #141b24 canvas — so the coachmark loses its "everything else recedes" effect. Two beats later the candidate-page frame opens and the backdrop flips to a light-cream fog. Two opposite scrim treatments alternate within one scripted run, on the single most presentation-facing surface in the app.
- **Root cause**: `SimSpotlight.tsx:77` hardcodes `boxShadow: "0 0 0 9999px rgba(28,25,23,0.28)"` — a literal light-theme ink that bypasses the token seam entirely, while `SimOfferFrame.tsx:44` uses `bg-ink/45`, which *does* resolve through `--color-ink` and therefore inverts to #f4efe3 (light) under `[data-theme="dark"]` (`app/globals.css:106`). Neither matches the shared `Modal`'s `bg-ink/30` weight either (`app/_components/Modal.tsx:151`). The design system's dark block remaps brand/neutral/status tokens (`globals.css:102-165`) but defines no scrim token, so each overlay improvised.
- **Impact**: In the register built "for creative users" the demo — the thing dark mode will be shown off with — reads broken: weak focus dim, then a flash-bang backdrop. Undermines the one-codebase-two-registers story the design commit just shipped.
- **Fix sketch**: Add a `--color-scrim` token next to the shadows in both theme blocks of `globals.css` (light: ink at ~30%; dark: a deepened canvas like `rgba(9,13,19,0.6)`, matching the sticker-shadow ink). Spotlight uses `boxShadow: "0 0 0 9999px var(--color-scrim)"`; SimOfferFrame swaps `bg-ink/45` for `bg-[var(--color-scrim)]`; migrate `Modal`/`CandidateDrawer` backdrops opportunistically per the recipes' migration rule. One token, every overlay agrees in both themes.

## 5. Surface the step-gate on the collapsed SimBar pill
- **Lens**: ui_perfectionist
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Category**: ui
- **File**: `app/features/simulation/SimBar.tsx:71`
- **Scenario**: Step mode is the demo default (`SimulationProvider.tsx:82`). A presenter starts the run, minimizes the footer to show the app full-bleed — and at the next phase boundary the demo silently freezes: `gate()` blocks awaiting Next (`SimulationProvider.tsx:152-161`), but the collapsed pill shows only "Pipeline simulation" with the same generic pulse dot it shows while playing (`SimBar.tsx:71-79`). The Next button exists only inside the expanded panel (`SimBar.tsx:50-52`). In front of a customer the demo just looks hung.
- **Root cause**: The pill renders no state beyond `sim.running`; `awaitingNext` (and `paused`) aren't reflected. The component's own header comment even promises "the run keeps playing behind the pill" (`SimBar.tsx:10-12`) — untrue under the default step mode.
- **Impact**: The most likely live-demo flow (default settings + minimized bar) dead-ends with no affordance; the presenter fumbles mid-pitch. Cheap to fix, high embarrassment saved.
- **Fix sketch**: When `sim.awaitingNext && collapsed`, restyle the pill as the primary action — coral fill, label "Next" + ChevronRight — and make its click call `sim.next()` instead of just expanding (keep a small expand chevron as the secondary hit target). Mirror the paused state with the moss "Resume" treatment. Reuses the exact button grammar already in the expanded bar (`SimBar.tsx:50-56`), so no new visual vocabulary.

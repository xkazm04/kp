## All 17 Critical findings — by theme

### Fairness & regulated-hiring correctness (6)
1. **Candidate Profile & Job Matching** — Saved analyses (and inline candidates) without a v2 profile silently score as "bau" — losing early-career scoring AND the fairness shield `app/_lib/match-candidate.ts:52`
2. **CV Extraction & Pipeline Services** — Credential gate & expiry are LLM-narrated only — no deterministic check for regulated roles `pipeline/jobfit/gemini.py:470 (and :471, pipeline.py:403-409)`
3. **Dev Case Pipeline (Python)** — Observed "Live Work Surface" path can never credit a probe — judgment silently suppressed at 0.8 confidence `pipeline/jobfit/devcase/process_events.py:100`
4. **Dev Submissions & Live Work Surface** — Live Work Surface is paste-blind — the authenticity moat has a hole `app/devcase/apply/[token]/LiveWorkSurface.tsx:112`
5. **Group Evaluation & Fairness** — "Fairness Check" measures weight-scheme robustness, not bias / adverse-impact fairness `app/features/sub_decisions/group-eval/FairnessPanel.tsx:6 (also types.ts:7, group-eval-run.ts:476-479)`
6. **Pipeline Board & Candidate Drawer** — Privacy posture is half-applied: the feed is anonymized, but full PII (incl. private recruiter notes) is served unauthenticated `app/_lib/pipeline-events-public.ts:5`

### Cross-tenant / workspace isolation (3)
7. **Analytics & Calibration Dashboards** — Workspace boundary applied inconsistently across the SAME dashboard `app/_lib/db/analytics.ts:131`
8. **Auth, Sessions & Workspace Tenancy** — Multi-tenancy is a fully-built "dark capability" gated only by an honor-system env flag `app/_lib/workspace-lock.ts:21`
9. **Landing & Marketing** — The entire public landing is never served in production `app/_lib/auth/devAuth.ts:23`

### Revenue leak / billing / monetization (3)
10. **Billing Engine & Webhooks** — Reordered `revoked`/`ended` event can downgrade a re-subscribed paying customer to free `app/_lib/billing/sync.ts:40`
11. **Communications & Inbound Channels** — Default deployment silently delivers nothing — recruiter Comms Center gives no relay-not-configured warning `app/_lib/comms.ts:97`
12. **Plans, Checkout & Billing UI** — "Switch to this plan" offers downgrades as a fresh checkout — contradicting the documented "downgrades via portal" design (double-subscription risk) `app/features/sub_billing/BillingTab.tsx:143`

### GDPR / audit / provenance / erasure (2)
13. **Dev Lifecycle, Cohort & Outcomes** — Probe-strength audit is advisory only — a case that "can't tell strong from naive" can still be approved and shipped `app/api/devcase/lifecycle/[id]/approve/route.ts:43`
14. **Privacy, Consent & Provenance** — Erasure leaves verbatim CV quotes behind — `evidenceTrace.*` is never scrubbed `app/_lib/consent.ts:129`

### Silent failure / edge cases / data integrity (2)
15. **Job Postings & Lifecycle** — Closed roles stay in the rematch corpus — two contradictory "is-live" predicates in one file `app/_lib/db/jobs.ts:274`
16. **Voice Interview** — whisper-1 is hardcoded, so the "final answer still in flight" recovery is dead code — the candidate's most decision-relevant closing answer can be silently dropped from the scored transcript `app/_lib/voice/openai.ts:98`

### Magic numbers / undocumented constants (1)
17. **Interview Scheduling, Prep & Rubric** — The whole company shares one global pool of 2 fixed slots/day — the assigned interviewer is ignored `app/_lib/schedule-slots.ts:18 (and app/_lib/schedule-store.ts:366)`

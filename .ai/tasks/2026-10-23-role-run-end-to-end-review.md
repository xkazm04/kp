# Role run end to end review (codebase-architecture-review)

The goal "one role runs end to end without a human step" stood with no review of the role-run engine and its producers as a whole. This read-only review asks what still stops one role on the seeded corpus from running from JD to decision with zero manual edits. The sequencer is sound and S0 to S2 have live producers, but the thread does not reach a candidate. No product surface starts a run or crosses a gate. A gate approval changes only the ledger: no board move, invite, offer or seal (the 2026-10-07 finding #3, whose "before any door" trigger has now fired). S3 to S6 have no producer. The goal's instrument measures the keyless floor rather than the live producers, and every reading on record was taken on the operator's own database rather than the seed. One keyless reading on a fresh seeded scratch database (`KP_SEED_DEMO=1`, needed since the 2026-10-22 default change) gives 3 of 4 branches at the offer draft, all declined as unrated, and 1 held for a person: not met. The review separates the deliberate human steps (three gates, holds, named approver) from the gaps, proposes the smallest honest measure (live runners on the seed, an interventions count, a committed reading), and ranks six deliveries. Two of them need an owner decision first. None needs a schema migration. No code changed.
